About Unity Hostel

Founded to offer students a secure, intellectually enriching, and modern collegiate home. Unity Hostel bridges university life with comfort, high-speed connectivity, and digital governance.

Quality Living

Architecturally planned rooms with cross-ventilation, ergonomic study spaces, daily housekeeping, and hygienic nutritious dining facilities.

Total Campus Safety

Biometric entrance scanners, dedicated round-the-clock female and male wardens, medical first-responder rooms, and 24x7 security personnel.

100% Digital Workflow

From paperless room selection to digital fee clearance, rapid maintenance ticketing, and automated night-out permission passes.

Comprehensive Campus Facilities

High-Speed Wi-Fi

Dedicated optical fiber network across all residential floors and common study halls.

Hygienic Mess & Cafe

Nutritious breakfast, lunch, high-tea, and dinner crafted with fresh ingredients daily.

Quiet Study Rooms

24-hour air-conditioned reading halls designed for uninterrupted exam preparation.

Laundry Services

Commercial washing machines and dedicated laundry dispatch cycles twice a week.

Medical Care

First-aid response room with visiting physicians and emergency hospital vehicle tie-ups.

Fitness Center

Cardio and weight training zone to foster physical wellness alongside academics.

Resident Code of Conduct & Guidelines

  • Curfew is strictly observed at 10:00 PM. Night out passes must be submitted online 24 hours prior.
  • Substance abuse, ragging, or disruptive conduct carries immediate expulsion with zero tolerance.
  • Quiet study hours are observed from 11:00 PM to 6:00 AM on all residential corridors.
  • Hostel property must be treated with care; damages will be charged to the resident's security deposit.
